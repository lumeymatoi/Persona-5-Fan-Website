Persona 5 (2016)

The original entry in the Persona 5 series. Players take on the role of a high school student who moves to Tokyo and becomes the leader of the Phantom Thieves of Hearts. Together, they enter the mysterious Metaverse to change the hearts of corrupt adults while balancing school, friendships, and everyday life.
