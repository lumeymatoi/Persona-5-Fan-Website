What Is Persona 5?

In Persona 5, you take on the role of a transfer student who arrives in Tokyo following a life-changing incident.In Persona 5, you take on the role of a transfer student who arrives in Tokyo following a life-changing incident. Alongside other young people who also feel wronged by society, he awakens a mysterious power known as a Persona.

Together, they form the Phantom Thieves of Hearts, a group determined to confront corrupt adults and transform the world around them. Amidst school life, friendships, and battles in a supernatural realm, every choice helps shape the path of the protagonist and his companions

Persona 5 blends RPG elements, dungeon crawling, and social simulation into a story about freedom, rebellion, and the pursuit of justice.
